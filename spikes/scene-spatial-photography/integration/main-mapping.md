The potential call site is Golden Flow Step 02 `UNDERSTAND REALITY`, before Target candidate generation. Main would launch a production-owned sweep capability, validate its manifest and candidate analysis, and govern any retained assets only with explicit consent.

Scene Spatial answers **which relative direction and which normalized image-plane area** appears compositionally useful. Live answers **how to align people and camera to an already chosen shot**. P1 does not replace Live, produce a `ShotDirection`, or claim a physical safe standing zone.

No Main source is changed and no spike UI becomes product navigation in P1.
