```text

Scene Sweep

SceneFrameSetV01 + SceneDirectionMapV01

PhotographyViewCandidateV01[] + PlacementCandidateV01[]
```

The potential call site is Golden Flow Step 02 `UNDERSTAND REALITY`, before Target candidate generation. Main would launch a production-owned sweep capability, validate its manifest and candidate analysis, and govern any retained assets only with explicit consent.

Scene Spatial P1 prepares **which relative directions and image-plane anchors remain candidates**. It does not choose the final photograph. Future P2 may add validated Photography Geometry / Spatial Evidence from the same scan. Live answers **how to align people and camera to an already validated and selected shot**. P1 does not replace Live, produce a `ShotDirection`, or claim a physical safe standing zone.

No Main source is changed and no spike UI becomes product navigation in P1.
